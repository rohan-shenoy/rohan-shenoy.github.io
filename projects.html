Projects

A selection of computer vision projects from CS180 at UC Berkeley.

Project 0: Becoming Friends with Your Camera

Exploring perspective distortion, perspective compression, and the dolly zoom effect through photography.

Project 1: The Prokudin-Gorskii Collection

Aligning color channels from historic glass plate negatives to reconstruct early color photographs.

Project 2: Fun with Filters and Frequencies

Image processing through convolution, edge detection, unsharp masking, hybrid images, and multi-resolution blending.

Project 3: Image Warping and Mosaicing

Computing homographies, warping images, and stitching panoramic mosaics with both manual and automatic feature matching.

Project 4: Neural Radiance Fields

Building a NeRF pipeline for 3D reconstruction from 2D images, including camera calibration and volume rendering.

Project 5: Fun with Diffusion Models

Exploring diffusion models for image generation, denoising, inpainting, visual anagrams, and training custom UNets on MNIST.
